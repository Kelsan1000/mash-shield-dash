# Mash Shield Dash (Unofficial FGO Fan Game)

A 2D stage-based auto-runner and defensive brawler starring **Mash Kyrielight**, covering Part 1 (*Observer on Timeless Temple*) across all 9 Singularities. Built mobile-first in **Godot 4 (.NET / C#)** as a standalone desktop and mobile app with a browser export.

## Core Features
* **Grounded Shield Combat:** No jumping—Mash stays planted on the ground and relies on **Guarding**, **Parrying**, **Shield Bashing**, and her **Noble Phantasm**.
* **Part 1 Story Progression:** Travel from Singularity F (Fuyuki) through the Final Singularity (Solomon), with each Singularity structured as a multi-stage World ending in a story boss.
* **FGO-Style Growth:** Level Mash from **1 to 80** (scaling her ATK and HP), upgrade her three skills with QP, unlock story-tied NP upgrades, and toggle Ascension stages or costumes.

## Tech Stack
* **Engine:** Godot 4 (.NET / C#)
* **Architecture:** .NET MVC-style separation (`Models/`, `Services/`, and Godot scene controllers) with zero external third-party packages.
